Sleep & Rest

Importance of Sleep & Rest

  1. Physical Recovery
    Sleep helps the body repair muscles, tissues, and cells.
  2. Mental Restoration
    Adequate rest enhances focus, memory, and emotional stability.
  3. Immune Support
    Good sleep strengthens the immune system.
  4. Better Mood
    Lack of sleep increases irritability and stress levels.
  5. Improved Productivity
    Proper rest boosts concentration and cognitive performance.

Tips for Better Sleep

  1. Maintain a Sleep Schedule
    Going to bed at the same time daily helps regulate your body clock.
  2. Limit Screen Time
    Avoid electronics before bed to reduce blue-light stimulation.
  3. Create a Relaxing Environment
    Keep your room dark, cool, and quiet.
  4. Avoid Heavy Meals Late
    Eating too close to bedtime interferes with sleep.
  5. Stay Active
    Regular exercise improves sleep quality.
