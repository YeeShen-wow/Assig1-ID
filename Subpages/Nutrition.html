Nutrition

Why Nutrition Matters

  1. Supports Growth & Development
    Nutrients help your body build and repair tissues.
  2. Boosts Immunity
    A balanced diet strengthens the immune system.
  3. Improves Energy Levels
    Eating well fuels your body for daily activities.
  4. Reduces Risk of Diseases
    Good nutrition lowers the risk of diabetes, heart disease, and obesity.
  5. Enhances Mental Function
    Proper nutrition improves focus, memory, and overall mood.

Tips for Healthy Eating

  1. Eat a Variety of Foods
    Include fruits, vegetables, proteins, and whole grains.
  2. Stay Hydrated
    Water helps digestion, circulation, and temperature regulation.
  3. Limit Sugary Foods
    Too much sugar contributes to weight gain and energy crashes.
  4. Watch Portion Sizes
    Eating the right amount prevents overeating.
  5. Avoid Processed Foods
    Choose fresh, unprocessed foods when possible.
