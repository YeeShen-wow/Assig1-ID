# Living Well Website
I made this made up website for my Interactive Development project. This website is basically for people who aspire to live a healthier life. My website allows them to contact professionals that can provide tips and ways to help them live better. And even if they are not comfortable with contacting professionals, there are videos and free advice that they can get from the different subpages of the website.

## Design Process
Since this website is for those who aspire to live their lives better. It is safe to assume that rough colours will not be the best choice so therefore I went with lighter colours that are easier on the eyes such as, green, blue and white. My choice of font being the Roboto Slab font makes it easy for people to read while not looking too plain and boring. This website allows users to see summarised tips about specific topics without having to do separate research for a niche topic that they might want. I made the pictures and icons pop out whenever you hover over them to provide clarity on what the user is currently selecting.

## Features
- My home page features the company's motto and a learn more button that leads to the About Us page. And below there are quick navigation buttons that lead to specific subpages.
- My about us page tells the user what the company does, the company's mission and why the company does what it does.
- The overview page gives users a quick summary of what each subpage is about without much details.
- The contact us page helps the user connect with the company so that the company will be able to refer the user to a professional that is suited for them.
- The health topics dropdown tab is for the subpages that contain more detail and more tips and videos about the specific topic.

### Existing Features
- For each page, theres a footer at the bottom for users to provide feedback 
- The homepage contains buttons that lead to subpages for quick and easy access
- The overview has a "learn more" link thhat leads to a specific subpage depending on what you read and want.
- Each subpage contains tips and advice on their respective topics
- The lifestyle choices subpage specifically contains an example routine beginners can try out to start living a healthy lifestyle.
- Users can fill out a form in the contact us page so the company would be able to review and refer them to a professional.

### Features Left to Implement

## Testing
- Footer feedback form
    1. Go to any page
    2. Try to submit while leaving either the Name or Message field empty and make sure it requires you to fill out the field
    3. Try to submit while leaving the email part empty and verify you need at least a "@" to submit

- Contact Us Form
    1. Go to Contact Us page
    2. Try to submit while leaving either the Name or Message field empty and make sure it requires you to fill out the field
    3. Try to submit while leaving the email part empty and verify you need at least a "@" to submit
    4. It should bring you to Outlook with your message ready to send.

- Health Topics Page
    1. Go to any page
    2. Hover over the Health Topics button in the navigation bar
    3. Click on any dropdown tab
    4. Ensure the subpage that you are brought to corresponds with the dropdown tab that you clicked

- Lifestyle Choices Subpage
    1. Go to the Lifestyle Choices subpage through the health topics button in the navigation bar
    2. Try to click and play each video

### Mobile view of the website
The minimum width of my website being 290px since anything smaller than that, the footer no longer fits the frame anymore. Once the website goes into a mobile view layout, a hamburger menu button would appear to open up a sidebar for the navigation tabs. I made this sidebar instead of keeping the normal navigation bar so that the website is navigatable with the use of one hand to accomodate to the small screens of phone users.
